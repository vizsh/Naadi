## Direction contract

THESIS: every tool call is a punched card the reader takes in; its holes are what the call read, the tape prints what it returned, and the owner's latch stops the chain before anything leaves. It refuses the chat log of steps.

OWN-WORLD: the landing page's reading head in matte steel and ink; step cards in slate pressboard with ink holes; a paper tape in a painted cell, one mono line per call typed on the run's clock; the latch an ink bar across the chain; amber and vermilion only for state.

STORY: press Run and cards feed one by one, the needles read each, the tape types each result; flip a what-if and the chain rewinds and reads again; the latch holds the drafts until Approve.
