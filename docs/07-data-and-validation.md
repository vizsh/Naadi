# 07 · Data and validation

NAADI is honest about where its numbers come from. This document separates three things: **the demo shed** (synthetic, deterministic), **the methods** (real, running live) and **the validation we still owe** (public datasets, then the field).

## The demo shed

Everything in the demo is generated by [`buildShed()`](../src/lib/model/shed.ts) from one seed (`20261117`), so every browser and the server see the same shed.

| Item | How it is made |
|---|---|
| Clock | "now" is Tue 17 Nov 2026, 12:00 IST; card *k* starts at 3 Nov 12:00 + 6k hours |
| Machines | six shuttle looms, a stone chakki, two pump sets (`MACHINES`) |
| Off cards | Sundays (weekly off) and a scheduled power cut on Thu 12 Nov, 14:00–18:00 (a card with under 3 h running is not scored) |
| Healthy degradation | a per-machine base level 0.012–0.024 with 8% log-normal noise |
| Faults | three profiles: an exponential $d = d_0 e^{b(t - t_0)}$ from an onset day, with 5% noise |
| Holes | per channel, per card: $p = 0.006 + A / \bigl(1 + e^{-(k - c)/w}\bigr)$, a logistic that rises along the fault |

| Profile | Onset | $d_0$ | $b$ /day | Channels that light up (amplitude, centre card) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loom 03 · outer race | day 5 | 0.04 | 0.19 | envelope (0.95, 24), kurtosis (0.9, 30), sound (0.85, 36), velocity (0.9, 40), temperature (0.8, 47), current (0.2, 48) |
| Pump 2 · cavitation | day 8 | 0.05 | 0.12 | sound (0.8, 34), current (0.6, 40), velocity (0.3, 46) |
| Loom 05 · belt | day 10 | 0.05 | 0.16 | 1× shaft (0.45, 42), current (0.35, 46) |

The channel order for the outer-race fault follows how bearings actually fail: the envelope sees the spall first, kurtosis rises as impacts sharpen, sound becomes audible, broadband velocity grows, and only near the end does the housing heat up.

What the demo does **not** pretend: the time-to-failure fit, the Monte Carlo, the rupees and the Bayesian ranking run on these synthetic cards live; they are not hard-coded.

## The envelope spectrum and the sounds

[`envelopeSpectrum()`](../src/lib/model/physics.ts) builds 0–400 Hz at 0.25 Hz resolution: a floor that falls with frequency, 0.6 dB of noise per bin, shaft-rate peaks in both traces, and in "now" the BPFO family (17.5, 12.5, 8 and 4.5 dB above floor) with FTF sidebands and a 1.2 dB broadband rise above 150 Hz. [`synth()`](../src/lib/audio/synth.ts) generates the matching sounds at 44.1 kHz: 100 Hz magnetic hum, shaft harmonics, noise, and for "now" a 3.2 kHz ring-down at every ball pass, with 1.2% jitter for slip.

## Public datasets that validate the method

| Dataset | What it is | What NAADI must show on it | Metric we will report |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NASA IMS** (Univ. of Cincinnati) | four bearings run to failure on a test rig, 20 kHz snapshots every 10 minutes over about a month | the change point fires well before failure, and the growth-rate prior (σ = 0.25 today) is calibrated | days of warning before failure; false alarms during the healthy period; calibration of P10/P50/P90 |
| **CWRU** bearing data | seeded inner-race, outer-race and ball faults at several sizes and loads, 12 kHz and 48 kHz | envelope analysis plus the rulebook names the right fault without training on faults | confusion matrix outer / inner / ball |
| **MIMII / DCASE Task 2** | normal and anomalous sounds of pumps, fans, valves and slide rails | the per-machine acoustic normal (log-mel autoencoder) separates anomalies | AUC and partial AUC per machine type against the DCASE baseline |
| **Physics simulator** (loom, chakki) | no public data exists for these machines | detection delay as fault severity grows | delay vs injected severity curve |

These results belong on a **model card** page in the app. Until they are computed, the app shows no accuracy claims at all.

## The field

The brief's boundary allows hardware to be prototyped or simulated with recorded signals. The field path after the hackathon:

1. **Two weeks of normal** on two looms with the ESP32 kit (vibration, sound, current, temperature), features computed on the device.
2. **Repair log**: every repair the owner already does becomes a labelled event that updates the rulebook priors.
3. **Owner interviews**: the six field questions in [docs/01](01-problem-and-users.md) fill the cost model with the shed's real margin, wages and lead times.

## Known limitations

- The degradation index in the demo is a direct draw, not yet an aggregate of live detector outputs.
- The Monte Carlo treats growth as exponential; spall growth can stall and jump. The ±25% prior is a placeholder for that risk until IMS calibrates it.
- Likelihood ratios in the rulebook are expert-set, cited to standard references (ISO 13373, bearing damage guides), not learned.
- Machine-drafted Hindi and Marathi strings need native review.
